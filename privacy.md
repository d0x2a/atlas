# Atlas: Paper Map Driving privacy policy

Effective 16 October 2026

Atlas: Paper Map Driving ("Atlas") is an iPhone app made by Dox2A Labs LLC ("we"). This policy
says what the app sends from your iPhone, why, and what we keep.

## In short

- Atlas has no account, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. We don't sell or share your
  information.
- To plan a route or search, the app sends your location and what you search for to our own
  servers, which use them only to answer that request.

## What the app sends, and why

**Routes.** When you plan a route, the app sends its start and destination to our routing
service at atlas-route.d0x2a.com. When you route from where you are, the start is your current
location. While you navigate, if you leave the route, the app sends your current location to get
a new one.

**Search.** When you search, the app sends what you type to our search service at
atlas-search.d0x2a.com. It also sends your location, or the middle of the map on screen when it
doesn't have your location, so nearer places come first.

**The map** is stored in the app, so showing it sends nothing.

Nothing else leaves your iPhone. The app never sends your name, contacts, photos, device
identifiers or advertising identifier.

## What we keep

Our services answer each request and don't store your routes or searches. Like any web service,
they keep short technical logs: the time, the IP address, and the address requested, which for a
search includes its text and the location sent with it. We use these logs only to keep the
services running and secure, delete them within 30 days, and never use them to identify you.

## Location on your iPhone

Atlas asks for your location while you use it, to show where you are and to guide you. During
navigation it keeps guiding when the screen is locked or another app is in front, and it stops
when navigation ends. You can turn location off in Settings › Privacy & Security › Location
Services. You can still use the map, and plan a route by long-pressing a start and a
destination.

Your settings, such as what routes avoid, voice guidance and Demo drive, are stored only on your
iPhone.

## Who else is involved

- **Cloudflare** carries and protects the requests to our services.
- **OVHcloud** hosts our servers.
- **OpenStreetMap** contributors made the map data. The app sends nothing to OpenStreetMap.
- **Apple** may share crash reports and usage data with us if you allow sharing with app
  developers, under Apple's privacy policy.
- **GitHub** hosts this website.

## Children

Atlas isn't directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect information from them.

## Your choices

We keep no profile of you, so beyond the short-lived logs above there is nothing of yours to see
or delete. You can still ask us about your data at
[contact@d0x2a.com](mailto:contact@d0x2a.com). If you live in California: we don't sell or share
personal information.

## Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and its date. If a version of the app starts
sending anything new, such as fetching live traffic from another provider, this page will say so
before that version is released.
