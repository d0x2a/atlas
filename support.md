# Atlas support

For questions, problems or ideas, email [contact@d0x2a.com](mailto:contact@d0x2a.com). Tell us
your iPhone model and iOS version, and what you were doing when it happened.

## Common questions

**Where does Atlas work?**
In Southern California: the Los Angeles basin and Orange County down to San Diego and the
border, east to San Bernardino, Riverside and Temecula. Outside that area the map is blank and
routes aren't available, but Demo drive still shows how navigation works.

**What does it need?**
An iPhone with iOS 17 or later, and an internet connection: the app fetches the map as you look
around, and search and routes come from our servers.

**Does the map work without a signal?**
Atlas keeps the parts of the map you've looked at. When you start navigating it also fetches the
map along your route, so a dead zone doesn't blank it. To have all of Southern California on your
iPhone, open the app's settings (the button above the locate button) and tap **Download Southern
California**: 375 MB, over Wi-Fi. Search and new routes still need a connection.

**Atlas doesn't show where I am.**
Atlas needs your location while you use it. On your iPhone, open Settings › Privacy & Security ›
Location Services › Atlas Nav, choose While Using the App, and turn on Precise Location. Without
your location you can still plan a route: after Directions, long-press where it starts.

**What does a long-press on the map do?**
It drops a dot there and shows the best guess at what's there: a place, an address or a road,
with what OpenStreetMap knows about it. Tap Directions for a route to the dot.

**How do I try navigation without driving?**
Tap the settings button, the three sliders at the bottom right, turn on Demo drive, and tap
Done. Plan a route and tap Start: Atlas drives it by itself with spoken instructions. End stops
it.

**How do I turn the voice off?**
Tap the speaker under the compass, on any screen. It shows a slash while guidance is quiet, and
stays that way until you tap it again. It's the same switch as Voice guidance in Atlas's
settings.

**Can routes avoid toll roads or highways?**
Yes. In Atlas's settings, turn on Avoid toll roads, Avoid highways or Avoid ferries. Routes keep
clear of them wherever there's another way; a trip that can't be made without one still gets a
route.

**Does Atlas use live traffic?**
No. Arrival times don't include live traffic, so allow extra time when roads are busy.

**A place, road or turn is missing or wrong.**
Atlas's map, search and routes come from [OpenStreetMap](https://www.openstreetmap.org), which
anyone can fix. [Report or fix it there](https://www.openstreetmap.org/fixthemap), or email us.
Fixes reach Atlas when its data is next updated.

**What does Atlas do with my data?**
It has no account, no ads and no tracking. The [privacy policy](privacy.md) says what the app
sends and what we keep.
